Blue and White Toile Bathroom Ideas: French Country Charm

ByDEMIRTAY · 2026-11-15

Toile de Jouy, the classic French print of little pastoral scenes in one color on a pale background, has been around since the 1700s and still looks fresh. In blue and white it feels calm, clean and a little nostalgic: perfect for a bathroom. Here is how to build the look without it turning into a museum.

1. Start with a toile shower curtain

The shower curtain is the largest piece of fabric in most bathrooms, which makes it the easiest way to bring in a pattern that would be expensive as wallpaper. A blue and white toile curtain sets the whole color story in one step.

2. Keep everything else simple

Toile is busy up close, so let it be the only large pattern. Surround it with plain surfaces:

  • Walls: soft white, warm cream or a very pale blue-grey.
  • Towels: crisp white with a blue stripe or border, or solid navy for contrast.
  • Bath mat: white, natural cotton or a simple blue stripe.

3. Blue and white toile bathroom accessories

Small accessories are where the theme becomes personal. Look for:

  • Blue and white ceramic soap dishes, toothbrush holders and lidded jars (Delft or chinoiserie styles work too).
  • A ginger jar or small vase with fresh hydrangeas or eucalyptus.
  • Brass or aged gold hooks and a round mirror for warmth.
  • A framed botanical or pastoral print in the same blue.

4. Mixing patterns the French country way

If you want a second pattern, change the scale and keep the color: a small gingham check, a thin stripe or a tiny floral in the same blue sits happily next to a large toile. Avoid a second large-scale print; it will fight the curtain.

5. Wood, wicker and linen

Natural textures stop blue and white from feeling cold. A wicker laundry basket, a wooden stool next to the tub and a linen hand towel give the room that lived-in farmhouse warmth.

6. Lighting and finishing touches

  • Warm white bulbs rather than cool daylight, so the whites look creamy.
  • Pleated or fabric lamp shades for a cottage feel.
  • A small stack of white towels tied with a blue ribbon on an open shelf.

Renter-friendly toile makeover

Toile curtain, two new towel sets, a blue and white soap dish and a vase of flowers: everything is removable and the bathroom looks completely different.
